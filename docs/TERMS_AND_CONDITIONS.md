# Brandspire POS — Owner Terms & Conditions

The production signup must require explicit acceptance of the approved English Terms & Conditions before submission.

Acceptance must store at minimum:
- terms_version
- accepted_at
- user_id
- organization_id when available
- request/audit metadata

Brandspire is the parent software-solutions brand/team. Brandspire POS is the billing/POS/business-management SaaS product.

Commercial fields such as trial duration, minimum subscription period, security deposit, printer arrangement and replacement charge must be database/Admin configurable rather than hardcoded.
